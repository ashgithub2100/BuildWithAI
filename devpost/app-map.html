LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY ENGINE

ReceiptSplit App Map & Architectural Tour

Complete technical blueprint of the 100% on-device expense ingestion, Hare-Niemeyer math engine, and Min-Cash-Flow graph debt solver.

⚡ 1. 3-Channel Ingestion Pipeline
  • Focus-Triggered Clipboard: window.onfocus reads copied text safely with hash de-duplication in useClipboardInterceptor.ts.
  • Global Hotkey Listener: Ctrl+V anywhere on screen parses clipboard into workbench.
  • Live Notification Stream: One-click and auto-streaming simulated WhatsApp and bank SMS messages.
  • Offline Tesseract OCR: HTML5 Canvas contrast binarizer + client Tesseract.js WASM worker.
chatExtractor.ts: parse natural language & Hinglish in <5ms
🧮 2. Hare-Niemeyer Math Engine
  • Integer Minor Units: All currency stored in integer paise (₹1.00 = 100p) to eliminate JavaScript float errors.
  • Largest Remainder Method: distributeLargestRemainder() distributes integer remainders deterministically.
  • Proportional Tax & Tip: Allocated strictly by each member's item subtotal weight.
  • Mathematical Proof: sum(sharesPaise) === expense.totalPaise holds to the exact paisa every single split.
moneyMath.ts: ₹1000 / 3 = ₹333.34 + ₹333.33 + ₹333.33 (0.00% err)
🕸️ 3. Min-Cash-Flow Graph Solver
  • Net Balances Pass: Computes net credit/debit for all members across unsettled expenses in Dexie IndexedDB.
  • Greedy Pairing: Recursively pairs maximum debtor with maximum creditor.
  • IOU Compression: Collapses complex webs (e.g. 12 group bills) into 2-3 direct payments.
  • Local Persistence: IndexedDB tables expenses and members (zero cloud DB writes).
debtGraph.ts: Collapses N-way debt chains into direct pairs
📱 4. NPCI UPI Settlement Engine
  • Authentic URI Builder: Formats upi://pay?pa=...&pn=...&am=...&cu=INR according to NPCI specifications.
  • High-Contrast SVG QR: Rendered in modal via qrcode.react for instant camera scanning.
  • 1-Click WhatsApp Export: Pre-formats monospace WhatsApp receipt breakdown with member shares.
  • Direct App Deep-Link: Launches Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM on mobile devices.
upiGenerator.ts: buildUpiUri() & formatWhatsAppSettlementText()
